# Packaging System

## Principles

- Premium but practical for a small maker business.
- Navy/neutral base with restrained cyan precision details.
- Clear product identification and QR-driven instructions.
- Subtle marks that can be printed, stickered, stamped, embossed, debossed, or laser engraved.
- Packaging should feel engineered and useful, not decorative for its own sake.

## Components

| Item | Format | Brand treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product box | Matte navy, graphite, or kraft with navy label | Small mark, product name, cyan rule line, material/process note |
| Shipping label | White or light gray label | Clean typography, small mark, no decorative clutter |
| Thank-you card | A6 or 4x6 | Navy front, cyan detail, concise message, support URL |
| Instruction card | A6 or 4x6 | Step hierarchy, QR code, care notes, contact path |
| Warranty card | A6 or 4x6 | Plain language, order/date field, support expectations |
| QR insert | Business-card size | Product page, care instructions, reorder/custom request link |
| Sticker | Die-cut mark or wordmark | One-color, reversed, and navy/cyan variants |
| Filament sample card | Small card with sample clip | Material, color, nozzle/temp notes, finish notes |
| Packaging tape | Navy mark repeat or cyan line | Use sparingly; avoid loud patterning |
| Small parts bag | Clear or frosted sleeve | Navy/cyan label, part name, orientation/care note |

## Copy Tone

Clear, useful, confident, and concise. Avoid hype and unsupported promises.

Good examples:

- Designed, printed, and checked in Rhode Island.
- Scan for care notes, files, and support.
- Functional parts with a clean engineered finish.
- Need a variation? Send a photo, sketch, or dimensions.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed engineering-grade strength.
- Fastest turnaround.
- Perfect tolerances.
- Overly cute maker puns.

## Visual Rules

- Use cyan as a rule line, registration mark, small icon, QR frame, or precision callout.
- Do not cover packaging in cyan.
- Keep logo marks small and confident.
- Leave negative space.
- Use the same product photography language as the site and social templates.

## Starter Insert Layout

Front:

```text
Robinson PrintWorks
[Product name]
Functional 3D printed part / accessory
```

Back:

```text
Care: Keep away from high heat unless material is specified for it.
Support: robinsonprintworks.com
QR: Product page / reorder / care notes
```

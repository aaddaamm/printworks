## Brand Fit Check

Before using a generated image, verify:

- Navy/neutral surfaces dominate the composition.
- Cyan appears as precision detail, not decoration.
- The product family feels consistent even without the logo.
- The image feels premium, functional, and approachable.
- No single printer or machine dominates the brand story.

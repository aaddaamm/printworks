How it works

Start with the problem. I’ll check whether it is printable, then we can work out material, timing, and price.

  1. Tell me what you need Describe the problem, or send a photo, broken part, sketch, or reference. Files are welcome but never expected. If a part needs an in-person fit check, request a site visit.
  2. Get clear next steps I’ll confirm material, timing, pickup or shipping, and cost.
  3. Approve the print Once approved, I print, inspect, and package it.

Common questions

Do I need a 3D file?

No. A photo, broken part, sketch, rough measurement, or simple description is plenty. STL, 3MF, and OBJ files are welcome if you have them.

What materials can you print?

Usually PLA, PETG, or TPU. ABS/ASA by request.

How much does a print cost?

Small simple prints are often $10–$25. Larger jobs get quoted.

Can you help if I only have an idea?

Yes. Send the idea, rough dimensions, photos, or a sketch.

Do you offer pickup or shipping?

Yes. Local pickup in Rhode Island. Small shipped orders by request.

What projects are not a good fit?

I’ll say no to unsafe, restricted, or unsuitable prints, and I’ll recommend another process when 3D printing is the wrong tool.
